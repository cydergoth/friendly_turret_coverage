# Star Valor mod to show coverage of friendly turrets

Note: This mod is only compatible if station DLC is installed

This mods add a toggle to the sector map and main view to overlay indicators for the fields of fire of friendly turrets.

When activated each turrent is scanned for the field of fire it has (360, 90, etc, blocked by asteroids or other stations) and the field of fire drawn as one or more arcs in a translucent highlight, except for
missile turrets which are drawn as a circle indicating maximum range. If a turret has two types of weapons they are treated as two superimposed turrets for display purposes

Where multiple turret arcs overlap, the highlight density is increased to indicate that

Different types of damage (beam, kinetic, missile, point defence, repair beam) have individual toggles which are displayed when the indicator master toggle is selected and will show turret indicators for that type of damage

One additional toggle displays each type of damage in a different color, or all types as a single color. Where damages of different types overlap, the colors are blended  

If there is a mouseover on a specific turret sweep a targetting line across the turret's field of fire at the same rate as the turret can traverse
